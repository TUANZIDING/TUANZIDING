## About · 关于我

I work in frontline emergency and trauma care, with clinical experience in polytrauma, pelvic fractures, and geriatric hip fractures. My doctoral research focuses on **radiation-induced skeletal muscle injury and repair**. I build open tools that connect clinical questions, medical education, reproducible research, and traceable evidence.

我的临床工作涉及多发伤、骨盆骨折与老年髋部骨折救治，博士研究聚焦**放射性骨骼肌损伤与修复**。我希望把临床经验转化为可理解的教学内容、可复现的研究流程与可追溯的医学 AI 工具。

- **Current research · 当前研究：** muscle stem cells, fibro-adipogenic progenitors (FAPs), immune–stromal interactions, fibrosis, and extracellular vesicles as a research direction. / 肌肉干细胞、FAPs、免疫与基质相互作用、纤维化，以及细胞外囊泡相关研究方向。
- **Clinical research · 临床研究：** trauma outcomes, perioperative hypotension, and musculoskeletal health. / 创伤结局、围术期低血压与肌肉骨骼健康。
- **Working principles · 工作原则：** traceable sources, explicit evidence limits, human review, and protection of patient privacy. / 来源可追溯、证据边界明确、人工复核、保护患者隐私。
